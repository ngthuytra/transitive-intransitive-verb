I. Put the verbs in the correct column

Choose whether each verb is transitive, intransitive, or can be either transitive or intransitive.

Suggested answers

Transitive: answer, park, give, buy, tell, explain, drop.

Intransitive: sleep, happen, arrive, live, rise, occur, exist.

Either: read, move, shake, ring, win, burn.

Some verbs can be used in more than one way depending on context. This exercise follows the intended grouping of the worksheet.

III. B. Change the sentences that have objects into the passive voice

Write the complete passive sentence. There are five sentences with direct objects.

Suggested answers
  1. The food will be prepared by the chef.
  2. The fire wasn't caused by lightning.
  3. The subway is ridden by thousands of people every day.
  4. All their belongings were burnt by the fire.
  5. His project will have been finished by John when we arrive next month.
The passive sentences may also be written without the agent (by + doer) when it is not important.
